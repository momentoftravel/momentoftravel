Our Team

Our Team

Besides our headquarters in Medina city — conveniently located within 10 minutes of Medina International Airport (MED) — Moment of Travel proudly and confidently covers all Saudi airports. Our team is comprised of trained and qualified professionals permanently located at the major airports within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia — DMM, RUH, MED, and JED — equipped with business tools for office use if a customer requires. Each team member is fully committed to meeting the highest standards of safety, security, and operational efficiency in aircraft handling. With a focus on compliance and excellence, our employees ensure reliable and seamless support to our aviation partners at every location, across all fields.
